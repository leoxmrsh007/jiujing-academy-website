body_en: |
  The sixty-four hexagrams of the Book of Changes speak of change, not constancy. Many mistake the study of the Changes as a way to court fortune and avoid misfortune — its deeper concern is teaching us that change itself is the constant: only by accepting every circumstance as fluid can one stand firm amid upheaval, rather than clinging to any single gain or loss.
  
  This autumn's seminar, led by the principal, will take "timing," "position," and "the incipient moment" as its framework — re-reading the Changes as a dynamic method of judgment for governance. To act before the time has come is rashness; to fail to respond once it has is stagnation. True wisdom lies in grasping the right measure amid change.
  
  Open to students and outside auditors alike; seats are limited. A Q&A session will follow — bring your real governance dilemmas to discuss with the principal directly.

《周易》六十四卦，言变不言常。世人多误以为易学教人趋吉避凶，实则其根本关切，在于教人认清「变」本身即是常道——唯有承认一切境遇皆流转不居，人才能在变局中站稳脚跟，而非执于一时之得失。

本次秋季会讲，山长将以「时」「位」「几」三字为纲，重讲《周易》如何为治理提供一套动态的判断方法：时未至而妄动，谓之躁；时已至而不应，谓之滞。真正的智慧，在于于变易中把握恰当的时机与分寸。

会讲面向书院学员与外部旁听者开放，名额有限，报名从速。届时将设问答环节，欢迎携实际治理困惑而来，与山长当面切磋。
