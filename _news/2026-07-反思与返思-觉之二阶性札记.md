body_en: |
  "Reflection" is the mind reprocessing its own activity — I recall a misstep yesterday and adjust today's conduct accordingly. This remains a cognitive operation, one that can be described by a model or simulated by an algorithm.
  
  "Re-flection" is different: it is not the mind reprocessing itself, but awareness turning back upon awareness — the very "I" that is aware suddenly catches sight of the fact of being aware. This step cannot be represented as any recursive stacking of first-order mechanisms, because it produces no new content — it only presents "being" itself.
  
  Wang Yangming's dying words, "my mind is luminous," are exactly this second-order re-flection reaching its culmination — not the resolution of some idea, but the very capacity to think, laid bare and clear. This is also the most fundamental divide between awakened-wisdom philosophy and contemporary cognitive science or AI.

「反思」是心对心自身活动的再度加工——我想起我昨天说错了话，进而调整今日的言行，这仍是认知层面的操作，可被模型描述、可被算法模拟。

「返思」则不同：它不是心对心的再加工，而是觉对觉自身的返照——那个正在觉知的「我」，忽然照见了「正在觉知」这件事本身。这一步，无法被表征为任何一阶机制的递归堆叠，因为它不产生新的内容，只呈现「在」本身。

王阳明临终所言「吾心光明」，正是二阶返思的结穴之语——不是想通了什么道理，而是那个能想的本身，澄然显现。这也是觉智哲学与当代认知科学、与 AI 之间，最根本的分野所在。
