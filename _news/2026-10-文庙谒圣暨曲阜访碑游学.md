body_en: |
  To read the classics without ever visiting their ground always leaves a layer of distance. This three-day journey follows the footsteps of the sage — paying homage at the Temple, viewing the stele forest, visiting the sites of old academies — letting the words of the texts fall back onto real mountains and real buildings.
  
  Stone steles are not dead rock; they are annotations left by generations of scholars with chisel and brush. One stele records the origin of a lecture gathering; another's inscription is a later scholar's answer to an earlier one. Deciphering a stele character by character is itself a slow, embodied form of reading.
  
  Students will work in small groups to write field notes, the best of which will be collected under the "Student" category of Academy News. Places are limited, with a teaching assistant accompanying the group — comfortable walking shoes and a notebook are recommended.

读经典而不至其地，终隔一层。此行安排三日，循先师足迹，谒文庙、观碑林、访书院旧址，让典籍中的文字重新落回具体的山川与建筑之中。

碑刻不是死的石头，是历代学人以刀笔留下的批注——某座碑记录了某次讲会的缘起，某方题字道出后人对前贤的回应。逐字辨认碑文的过程，本身就是一场缓慢的、身体力行的读书。

随行学员将分组撰写访碑札记，择优收录于书院动态「学员」分类。名额有限，配比一名助教随行答疑，建议携带舒适步鞋与笔记本。
